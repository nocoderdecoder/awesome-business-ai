## General AI Assistants

The starting point. These are the tools every business professional should know.

- [ChatGPT](https://chat.openai.com) — OpenAI's flagship AI for drafting, analysis, research, and task automation across any business function.
- [Claude](https://claude.ai) — Anthropic's AI, particularly strong for long-document analysis, nuanced writing, and reasoning tasks in compliance-sensitive environments.
- [Google Gemini](https://gemini.google.com) — Google's multimodal AI deeply integrated with Workspace (Docs, Gmail, Sheets), enabling AI inside tools your team already uses.
- [Microsoft Copilot](https://copilot.microsoft.com) — Microsoft's AI embedded across Word, Excel, Outlook, and Teams for generating reports, summarizing emails, and automating M365 workflows.
- [Perplexity AI](https://www.perplexity.ai) — Research-focused AI that answers questions with real-time web citations, ideal for competitive research and fact-checking.
- [Grok](https://grok.x.ai) — xAI's assistant with real-time access to X/Twitter data, useful for trend monitoring and social media intelligence.
- [Meta AI](https://www.meta.ai) — Meta's AI built into WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook, increasingly used for customer engagement and quick business queries.
- [Poe](https://poe.com) — Aggregator giving access to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and others in one interface, useful for comparing outputs.

- [Clari](https://www.clari.com) — AI-driven pipeline forecasting and deal inspection platform for revenue leaders.

## Meetings & Transcription

Save every meeting. Never write notes again.

- [Otter.ai](https://otter.ai) — AI meeting notetaker with high transcription accuracy, speaker identification, and searchable archives for Zoom, Teams, and Meet.
- [Fireflies.ai](https://fireflies.ai) — Records and transcribes meetings then automatically pushes summaries and action items to Salesforce, Asana, Slack, and 40+ tools.
- [Fathom](https://fathom.video) — Free AI meeting recorder with real-time highlight marking and instant summaries — the best free option for Zoom-focused teams.
- [tl;dv](https://tldv.io) — AI meeting recorder that lets you create shareable video clips and timestamped highlights from Zoom, Meet, and Teams.
- [Read.ai](https://www.read.ai) — Meeting analytics that scores engagement, tracks attention, and provides speaker analytics alongside transcription and summaries.
- [Avoma](https://www.avoma.com) — End-to-end meeting platform for revenue teams combining AI notes, CRM sync, coaching, and conversation analytics.
- [Loom AI](https://www.loom.com) — Async video messaging with AI-generated summaries, chapters, and action items for recorded walkthroughs and updates.
- [Grain](https://grain.com) — Records and highlights key moments from sales calls, syncing clips and summaries to HubSpot and Salesforce automatically.

## Writing & Editing

- [Grammarly Business](https://www.grammarly.com) — AI writing assistant that catches errors, improves clarity, adjusts tone, and enforces brand style guides across all team writing.
- [Jasper](https://www.jasper.ai) — Enterprise AI writer with 50+ templates for blogs, emails, ads, and social posts, trained to maintain brand voice at scale.
- [Writer](https://writer.com) — Enterprise AI with company-specific knowledge, style enforcement, and compliance checks built for regulated industries.
- [Notion AI](https://www.notion.com/product/ai) — Built into Notion's workspace to draft documents, summarize pages, extract action items, and generate content without leaving your notes.
- [Wordtune](https://www.wordtune.com) — Rewrites, condenses, or expands your sentences while preserving meaning, especially useful for non-native English speakers.
- [Hemingway Editor](https://hemingwayapp.com) — Highlights overly complex sentences, passive voice, and readability issues to help business writers communicate with clarity.
- [Lex](https://lex.page) — AI-powered writing tool designed for long-form business documents, essays, and reports with an uncluttered interface.

- [Plus AI](https://www.plusdocs.com) — AI presentation tool that works inside Google Slides and PowerPoint to generate, edit, and redesign decks from prompts.

## Data & Analytics

Turn data into decisions without needing SQL or a data analyst.

- [Tableau Pulse](https://www.tableau.com) — AI-generated natural-language insights and proactive anomaly detection on top of your Tableau dashboards.
- [Microsoft Power BI Copilot](https://powerbi.microsoft.com) — Generates reports, writes DAX formulas, and creates dashboards from natural language prompts inside the M365 ecosystem.
- [ThoughtSpot](https://www.thoughtspot.com) — Agentic analytics with a Google-like search bar on top of enterprise data, letting business users query data without SQL.
- [Julius AI](https://julius.ai) — Upload CSVs or connect to databases and ask questions in plain English — returns charts and written analysis instantly.
- [Polymer](https://www.polymersearch.com) — No-code tool that turns spreadsheets and CSVs into interactive dashboards and visualizations without technical skills.
- [Domo](https://www.domo.com) — Cloud BI platform with AI-powered insights, automated anomaly alerts, and conversational analytics for business leaders.
- [Rows](https://rows.com) — Spreadsheet tool with a built-in AI analyst that answers questions about your data and automates data imports from APIs.

- [Workato](https://www.workato.com) — Enterprise integration and automation platform with an AI co-pilot for building complex cross-system workflows.
- [Lindy](https://www.lindy.ai) — AI agent builder for personal assistants that handle email, scheduling, research, and cross-app workflows without code.
- [n8n](https://n8n.io) — Open-source automation platform with LangChain integration and 70+ AI nodes for teams that want full control of their workflow infrastructure.

## Prompt Libraries & Guides

Better prompts = better outputs.

- [Anthropic Prompt Library](https://www.anthropic.com/prompt-library) — Anthropic's official library of Claude prompts organized by business use case: customer service, analysis, writing, and more.
- [OpenAI Prompt Engineering Guide](https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/prompt-engineering) — Official guide with tested strategies for getting consistently better outputs from GPT-4 and later models.
- [AIPRM](https://www.aiprm.com) — Chrome extension integrating curated prompt templates directly into ChatGPT, with strong coverage of SEO, marketing, and sales tasks.
- [God of Prompt](https://www.godofprompt.ai) — Library of 30,000+ business-focused prompts for ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney, and Gemini organized by role and use case.
- [PromptBase](https://promptbase.com) — Marketplace for buying and selling optimized prompts for ChatGPT, Midjourney, and other AI tools.
- [FlowGPT](https://flowgpt.com) — Community platform for sharing and discovering prompts with ratings and use-case filters.
- [Learn Prompting](https://learnprompting.org) — Free, open-source guide to prompt engineering from basics to advanced techniques, with business-relevant examples.
