# Awesome Business AI [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

**A curated collection of useful AI tools, learning resources and practical guides for business.**

Made for founders, small-business owners, marketers, salespeople, managers, analysts and anyone curious about using AI at work. **No coding required to use this directory.**

Start with what you want to do, not with a tool name. Each entry explains a possible business use in plain English. This list is selective, not a ranking or product endorsement.

**First time using AI for work?** Start with [your first useful task](docs/start-here.md) or browse [everyday business examples](docs/business-use-cases.md).

## Getting started

1. **Pick one small task:** for example, writing an email, summarizing approved notes or organizing an outline.
2. **Try a suitable tool:** use made-up information at first, or information you have permission to share.
3. **Check its work:** review facts, figures, sources and promises before using anything.
4. **Decide whether it helps:** count time spent correcting the output, not just time spent generating it.

Read the [step-by-step first-task guide](docs/start-here.md) for a sample request you can copy.

## Browse tools and resources

This directory currently contains **37 resources**. Links lead to official product pages or original publishers. Features, access and prices change. Some business tools need a paid plan, administrator approval or technical support.

### AI assistants

Draft documents, plan work and think through everyday business questions.

- **[ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/overview/)** - Draft emails, plans and documents, then check the facts.
- **[Claude](https://claude.com/product/overview)** - Work with documents and produce structured first drafts.
- **[Google Workspace AI](https://workspace.google.com/solutions/ai/)** - Explore AI features for email, documents and other Google Workspace tasks.
- **[Microsoft Copilot for business](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/solutions/business)** - Explore AI assistance in Microsoft business applications.

### Research and company knowledge

Find information, explore documents and check sources.

- **[Perplexity Enterprise](https://www.perplexity.ai/enterprise)** - Research questions using sources you can open and verify.
- **[Notion AI](https://www.notion.com/product/ai)** - Search and draft information from a team's Notion workspace.
- **[Stanford AI Index](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index)** - Read a research-based report on the development and adoption of AI.

### Writing and translation

Improve business writing and communicate in other languages.

- **[Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com/ai)** - Check wording, clarity and tone before sharing written work.
- **[DeepL Translator](https://www.deepl.com/en/translator)** - Translate emails, documents and business text.

### Marketing, design and video

Draft campaigns, presentations, graphics and recordings.

- **[Jasper](https://www.jasper.ai/)** - Draft marketing copy from an approved campaign brief.
- **[Canva AI](https://www.canva.com/canva-ai/)** - Create graphics and visual drafts for marketing or presentations.
- **[Gamma](https://gamma.app/)** - Turn outlines into draft presentations and documents.
- **[Descript](https://www.descript.com/)** - Edit videos, audio, transcripts and captions.

### Sales and customer relationships

Understand sales conversations and organize customer information.

- **[Gong](https://www.gong.io/)** - Review sales conversations and related customer information.
- **[HubSpot AI](https://www.hubspot.com/products/artificial-intelligence)** - Explore AI-supported sales, marketing and CRM tasks.

### Customer support

Explore tools for drafting and checking responses to customers.

- **[Fin](https://fin.ai/)** - Explore automated replies to customers using approved support information.
- **[Zendesk AI](https://www.zendesk.com/ai/)** - Explore AI features for customer-service workflows.

### Meetings and notes

Turn permitted recordings and discussions into notes and follow-ups.

- **[Otter](https://otter.ai/)** - Capture transcripts and draft meeting notes.
- **[Fireflies](https://fireflies.ai/)** - Organize meeting summaries and action items.

### Finance, planning and reporting

Explore plans, metrics and explanations of business results.

- **[Pigment](https://www.pigment.com/)** - Explore business planning and scenario comparisons.
- **[Datarails](https://www.datarails.com/)** - Explore spreadsheet-based finance planning and reporting.
- **[Tableau Pulse](https://www.tableau.com/products/tableau-pulse)** - Review summaries and changes in defined business metrics.
- **[Copilot for Power BI](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/create-reports/copilot-introduction)** - Read Microsoft's guide to AI-assisted reporting in Power BI.

### Projects and automation

Organize tasks and connect recurring work between applications.

- **[Asana AI](https://asana.com/product/ai)** - Organize projects, tasks and status updates.
- **[Airtable AI](https://www.airtable.com/platform/ai)** - Work with structured business records and workflows.
- **[Zapier AI](https://zapier.com/ai)** - Connect routine tasks across apps, including AI-supported steps.
- **[Make](https://www.make.com/)** - Build visual workflows between business tools.
- **[n8n](https://n8n.io/)** - Create customizable workflows; some setups need technical help.

### Hiring and people

Explore recruiting administration while keeping people responsible for decisions.

- **[Workable](https://www.workable.com/)** - Explore tools for recruiting administration and hiring workflows.

### Online stores

Get help with everyday tasks involved in running an online shop.

- **[Shopify Sidekick](https://www.shopify.com/sidekick)** - Explore support for product content and store-management tasks.

### Learn the basics

Introductory courses and learning resources for non-engineers.

- **[Elements of AI](https://course.elementsofai.com/)** - Take a beginner course on what AI can and cannot do.
- **[AI for Everyone](https://www.coursera.org/learn/ai-for-everyone)** - Learn how to recognize AI opportunities in an organization.
- **[Grow with Google AI training](https://grow.google/ai/)** - Browse short courses and resources for everyday AI skills.
- **[Microsoft AI learning hub](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/ai/)** - Find AI learning resources from introductory to advanced levels.

### Safe and responsible use

Understand common risks and review questions before using AI at work.

- **[NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)** - Read a framework for assessing AI risks and responsibilities.
- **[NIST AI RMF Playbook](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework/nist-ai-rmf-playbook)** - Browse suggested actions for managing risks during AI projects.
- **[OWASP Top 10 for LLM and Gen AI applications](https://genai.owasp.org/llm-top-10/)** - Learn about common risks when connecting AI to business systems.

## How this list is maintained

We choose resources that have a clear business use and an official public source. A listing does not mean we have tested the product, approved its security, verified its pricing or independently compared it with alternatives. Before uploading business information, check your organization's policies and the provider's current terms. Read the [selection method](docs/methodology.md).
